# English localization style

Write idiomatic US English. Keep the speaker’s intent, characterization, humor and emotional tone. When a joke depends on a misreading, make the setup and its later payoff work together in English.

## Names players see

In character creation, tooltips and dialogue, use **Aegis Fighter**, **Twin Striker**, **Blast Archer**, **Spell Caster**, **Heavy Smasher**, **Blitz Lancer** and **Beat Performer**.

Use **Koz** for the Aegis Fighter instructor and **Millie** for the Reclamation Bureau receptionist. **Aeryn** and **Tyris** travel together. Use **Lady Aeryn** when Tyris addresses her formally. **Einrain** is the Oracle Priestess.

Use **Regnas** for the planet and **Regnas Store** for the shop. In Asterleeds, use **City Gate Square** and **Reclamation Bureau**. Early quest directions should match the map labels **Cliff Ruins**, **Asteria Plains** and **Lakerid Mine**.

## Directions and interface text

Name the character who gives an item and the character who receives it. Tell the player which action counts, where to take it, and how many times to do it when the original specifies a count. Distinguish a skill’s charge time, hit interval, effect duration and cooldown.

Keep menu labels short enough to read in the original space. Preserve the game’s placeholders, markup, meaningful line breaks and typed row IDs. Check repeated terms in their actual contexts before editing them together. Keep the original voices with English subtitles.
